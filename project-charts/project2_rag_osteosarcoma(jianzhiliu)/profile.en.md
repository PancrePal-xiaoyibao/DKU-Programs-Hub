- Leading sarcoma diagnosis and treatment center in China
- Experienced sarcoma specialist team
- Providing authoritative medical guidance and content review

### 💼 **JueQian Medical**

*Refined Operations*
JueQian Medical has maintained deep collaboration with the Soft Tissue Sarcoma MDT team since 2019, managing and maintaining the【Sarcoma Oasis】public platform and patient community, providing free and charitable services to patients with no commercial fees.
